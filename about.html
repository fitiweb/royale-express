Our Story

Royale Express was born in Lagos, Nigeria, from a simple observation: customers deserve to see where their parcel is, get a professional waybill with QR code, and print receipts — just like DHL.

Today, we operate a sorting & distribution center with barcode scanners, CCTV, and a 5-person verification team. Every shipment gets a tracking number RE-... and waybill WB-... with full sender/receiver addresses, shipment date/time, and expected delivery.
